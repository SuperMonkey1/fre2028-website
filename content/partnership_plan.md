# Fré Leys: LA 2028 Team & Sponsorship Plan

## 🎯 Core Objectives

This plan is designed to achieve three primary goals:

* **1. Performance Goal:** To secure the necessary financial, professional, and performance-based support to successfully train for and qualify for the LA 2028 Paralympics.
* **2. Advocacy Goal:** To build a dedicated team (of partners, sponsors, and professionals) to actively promote the **Paralympic Games of 2028** throughout **Leuven and Flanders**.
* **3. Legacy Goal:** To become the **first-ever Paralympian from Leuven**, using this personal journey as a platform to inspire the community and create lasting awareness for Paralympic sports.

This plan outlines the four key groups that will form this complete support system.

---

## 1. The Financial Partners: The Leuven 25 Support Circle

* **Goal:** To create a solid financial operating budget (~€25,000 - €30,000 / year) allowing Fré to train, recover, and operate full-time as a professional athlete.
* **Target:** Exactly **25 partner companies in Leuven**.
* **The Ask:** **€100 per month** (€1,200 per year).
* **The Reality / Context:**
    * Paraclimbing in Belgium receives minimal government subsidies; even international competition travel is not fully covered (e.g. ~€3,500 out-of-pocket for 2026 competitions).
    * In surrounding countries (France, Germany, UK), competitors are fully funded and professionally supported.
    * A 100% preparation for LA 2028 is not combinable with a regular job; training, recovery, travel, and managing communications is a full-time commitment.
* **Focus on Leuven:** Exclusively Leuven-based companies. This keeps logistics lean and concentrates community impact and Paralympic visibility where it matters most.
* **Campaign Push:** A focused 3-month push across media and business networks in Leuven.
* **The Return for Partners:**
    * **Brand Visibility:** Logo featured on the official campaign poster, website, and competition/campaign T-shirt.
    * **Annual Partner Event:** 1x per year exclusive partner event featuring a climbing initiation, film screening, and keynote talk (on resilience, innovation, and an engineer's approach to elite sports).
    * **Community Activations:** Presence/activation at Leuven Kerstmarkt 2027.
    * **Poster 2028:** Distributed to every mailbox in Leuven and every school.
    * **Advocacy Impact:** Active promotion of paraclimbing through [paraclimbing.be](https://paraclimbing.be) and the Paralympic Games.

---

## 2. Strategic Partners (Leuven Pro Bono & Media Services)

* **Goal:** To acquire professional services and media firepower to run the 3-month push and campaign effectively without direct financial strain on athletic budgets.
* **Target:** Leuven-based agencies, studios, and skilled professionals.
* **Profile:**
    * PR & Media agencies
    * Content creators (videographers, photographers, storytellers)
    * Legal and administrative support
* **The Ask:** Pro bono services and expertise.
* **The Return:**
    * Official Strategic Partner status and prominent visibility across website, media releases, and event stages.
    * Tangible involvement in putting Leuven and inclusive elite sports on the world map.

---

## 3. The Performance Team (Expert Support)

* **Goal:** The dedicated sports science and medical team responsible for Frederik's athletic, physical, and mental peak performance for Los Angeles 2028.
* **Team Members:**
    * Head Coach & Climbing Trainers
    * Physiotherapist (Kine) & Rehab Specialists
    * Sports Psychologist & Mental Coach
    * Nutritionist
    * Climbing & Mountaineering Federation / G-Sport Flanders
