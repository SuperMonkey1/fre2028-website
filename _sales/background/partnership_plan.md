# Fré Leys: LA 2028 Team & Sponsorship Plan
## Subtitle: *"Engineering my way to the Paralympics in 2028"*

## 🎯 Core Objectives & The Engineer-Athlete Vision

This plan is built on a unique convergence of **elite Paralympic sports, engineering innovation, and the Leuven high-tech ecosystem**:

* **1. The Engineer-Athlete Narrative:** Dr. Ir. Frederik Leys holds a PhD in Mechanical Engineering (KU Leuven) and is a 2x World Cup Gold paraclimber. As a passionate maker and tinkerer, Fré approaches athletic performance as an engineering and optimization challenge—developing custom training tools, sensor data analytics, biomechanical feedback, and testing innovative equipment.
* **2. The Leuven Innovation Powerhouse:** Leuven is the European Capital of Innovation and one of the world's most concentrated deep tech, health tech, and advanced engineering ecosystems (KU Leuven, Imec, UZ Leuven, Arenberg Science Park, Haasrode Research Park). This project leverages Leuven's unique capacity to engineer solutions to win Paralympic Gold.
* **3. Performance Goal:** To secure the financial, technical, and sport-scientific backing needed to qualify for and win Gold at the **LA 2028 Paralympics** (where paraclimbing makes its historic Paralympic debut).
* **4. Advocacy & Community Goal:** To build a dedicated consortium of partners to actively promote the **Paralympic Games of 2028** throughout **Leuven and Flanders** via [paraclimbing.be](https://paraclimbing.be).
* **5. Legacy Goal:** To become the **first-ever Paralympian from Leuven**, proving that local engineering, technology, and resilience can conquer the global stage.

---

## 1. The Financial & Innovation Partners: The Leuven 25 Support Circle
### *"25 Innovatieve Leuvense Bedrijven op weg naar Goud"*

* **Goal:** To create a solid financial and technical operating foundation (~€25,000 - €30,000 / year) allowing Fré to train, test, recover, and operate full-time as a professional athlete.
* **Target:** Exactly **25 innovative engineering, deep-tech, and forward-thinking companies in Leuven**.
* **The Core Pitch:** *"I am an engineer, and my Paralympic preparation, adaptive body, and custom equipment serve as an R&D testbed for Leuven innovation. Together, we engineer our way to Paralympic Gold in LA 2028."*
* **The Ask:** **€100 per month** (€1,200 per year).
* **The Context / Why this is essential:**
    * Paraclimbing in Belgium receives minimal government subsidies; international competition travel is not fully covered (e.g. ~€3,500 out-of-pocket for 2026 competitions).
    * In neighboring countries (France, Germany, UK), competitors are fully funded and supported.
    * A 100% preparation for LA 2028 is not combinable with a regular job; training, biomechanical R&D, recovery, travel, and managing communications is a full-time commitment.
* **Focus on Leuven Innovation:** Concentrating on Leuven-based companies keeps collaboration tight, provides a showcase for local high-tech excellence, and focuses community pride where it matters most.
* **Campaign Push:** A focused 3-month push across media, tech networks, and business circles in Leuven.
* **The Return for Partners:**
    * **Brand Visibility:** Logo featured on the official campaign poster, website (fre2028.la), and competition/campaign apparel.
    * **Annual Partner Event:** 1x per year exclusive partner event featuring a climbing initiation, film screening, and keynote talk (on resilience, innovation, and an engineer's approach to elite sports).
    * **Community Activations:** Presence/activation at Leuven Kerstmarkt 2027.
    * **Poster 2028:** Distributed to every mailbox in Leuven and every school.
    * **Innovation Showcase & Advocacy:** Association with groundbreaking engineering in sports and active promotion of paraclimbing through [paraclimbing.be](https://paraclimbing.be).

## 2. Strategic Partners (Leuven Pro Bono & Media Services)

* **Goal:** To acquire professional services and media firepower to run the 3-month push and campaign effectively without direct financial strain on athletic budgets.
* **Target:** Leuven-based agencies, studios, and skilled professionals.
* **Profile:**
    * PR & Media agencies
    * Content creators (videographers, photographers, storytellers)
    * Tech & prototyping collaborators (hardware, software, data analytics)
    * Legal and administrative support
* **The Ask:** Pro bono services, technical expertise, or media distribution.
* **The Return:**
    * Official Strategic Partner status and prominent visibility across website, media releases, and event stages.
    * Tangible involvement in putting Leuven's innovation and inclusive elite sports on the world map.
