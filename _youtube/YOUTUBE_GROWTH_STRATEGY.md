**Hard constraint:** first products on sale in **mid-November 2026** to catch the Christmas season. Sloper King's first batch is already made and teased on Instagram.

This document sits on top of the existing strategy files and does not replace them. [FRE2028_YOUTUBE_CONTENT_STRATEGY.md](FRE2028_YOUTUBE_CONTENT_STRATEGY.md) covers *what* to make and how to package it. ## 1. Diagnosis: what I found

**Strengths (rare ones):**
- A story with a deadline. LA 2028 is the finale, and almost no channel has one.
- Credibility that can't be copied: PhD engineer, 2× World Cup gold, IFSC athlete rep, and a person who builds his own gear.
- Real products with real logistics already live: Sloper King (shop, margins, gym plan) and FingerPrint (a generator that turns your own finger lengths into a custom crimp edge, €49, newsletter-gated free file).
- A credible friend in the top tier (Jakob Schubert) and a deep content backlog (33 ideas, 6 briefs).

**Weaknesses and risks:**
1. **Too many ideas, too little sequence.** The backlog is excellent and there is no ranking. A zero-subscriber channel lives or dies on its first 5 videos.
2. **Audience mismatch for selling.** The master strategy says "70% maker/engineering, 30% climbing" to reach a bigger audience. That is good for reach but bad for sales. Sloper King and FingerPrint are bought by **climbers**, not by r/3Dprinting. Makers watch, climbers buy. I shift the ratio (see section 3).
3. **The workload is not sustainable next to an elite training load.** Flagship builds take 20–50 hours each. The Tier A/B/C model already in your docs is right. I make it a hard cadence rule.
4. **The funnel must be finished before mid-November** (section 6): order email and Stripe live mode on FingerPrint, and real photos and video on the product pages (they are placeholders today).
5. **The account base is small:** Instagram ~5k, newsletter ~100. That is enough to seed, not enough to launch on. The first 3 videos need external seeding.

## 3. The funnel: how views become euros

```
YouTube long video  ─┐
Shorts / Reels      ─┼─►  Curiosity  ─►  fre2028.la (free tool or product page)
Gyms (QR cards)     ─┘                         │
                                    ┌──────────┴───────────┐
                                    ▼                      ▼
                         Free: FingerPrint file      Buy: Sloper King / FingerPrint
                         (email required)                   │
                                    │                       ▼
                                    └──►  NEWSLETTER  ──►  next product, LA updates, sponsors
```

**Principle: the email list is the real asset, not the subscriber count.** YouTube decides who sees you; the list is the only audience you own. Every video has exactly one primary link, and it always goes somewhere that captures an email or takes an order.

**Per-video sales mechanics (every long video):**
- One soft product moment at ~60–70% of the video, right after the proof moment (the "two-path" pitch you already wrote: free file, or ready-made set).
- Pinned comment + first line of the description: one link, one sentence.
- End screen leads to the *next* video in the same product family, not a random upload.
- A Short from each video that points to the product page in the profile link.

**Product ladder (what to sell as the audience grows):**

| Rung | Product | Price | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | FingerPrint generator and file (email gate) | €0 | Lead magnet and viral tool |
| Entry | Sloper King single | €24.95 | Impulse buy |
| Core | Sloper King pair / FingerPrint set | €44.95 / €49 | Main revenue |
| Bundle | "Climber's Christmas Kit": FingerPrint + Sloper King pair (separate sum €93.95; your price call) | tbd | Raises order value, easy gift |
| Premium | Custom (name/logo engraving, custom depth: already in your ideas list) | +€10–20 | Margin |
| Wholesale | Gym consignment (8-set boxes, already planned) | €23/set | Local distribution |
| Support | Pay-what-you-want contribution (already built) | free choice | Funds the LA campaign |

Realistic sales model (**assumptions, to be replaced by real data after video 3**): a niche training product with a direct call-to-action tends to give roughly **1–3 orders and 5–15 email sign-ups per 1,000 views** of a video that features it. A 30k-view product video would then yield about 30–90 orders (~€1.5–4k revenue) and 150–450 emails. A later launch to a warm email list typically converts better than a cold video. That is why sign-ups matter more than likes.

## 4. Launch order (rebuilt around the Christmas window)

The deadline decides the order: **products first, flagship later.** Jakob's custom grip needs his schedule, a 3D scan and a 15–20 minute edit. It cannot be done safely by mid-November, and it sells nothing directly. It becomes the **January flagship** ("new year, new training"), when the channel already has a product audience to show it to.

| # | Video | Publish | Tier | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Sloper King**: the WK 2025 failure → sloper physics → the tool → real gym clips | **~Thu 12 Nov** | B | **Launch product.** Batch exists, shop exists, story is your strongest. |
| **2** | **FingerPrint**: "Your fingers aren't the same length, so I made a crimp for *yours*" | **~Thu 26 Nov** (Black Friday is 27 Nov) | B | **Second product**, interactive tool (shareable), perfect gift angle. |
| **3** | **Can a 3D print hold 200 kg?** | **~Thu 10 Dec** | B | Last pre-Christmas push, and the proof that both products are safe. Cheap to make. |
| **4** | **Custom grip for Jakob Schubert** | **Jan 2027** | A | Flagship, audience expansion; FingerPrint is "the same idea for everyone". |
| **5** | History of indoor climbing / Pico | Feb 2027 | A | Evergreen, shows storytelling range. |
| **6** | Transparent climbing wall | Mar 2027 | A | Reach and press. |
| later | Bionic foot, uneven hangboard, force sensors | Around competitions | A/B | Reach and depth. |

Notes:
- **Two-video bank instead of three.** Videos 1 and 2 must be fully edited before 12 Nov. Video 3 is shot while 1 and 2 are live.
- **Jakob:** use the weeks until January to agree his launch-day role (collab Reel plus a mention) and a shoot date. If he can shoot earlier, film it, but still publish it after the products' Christmas window.
- **Fallback:** if video 2 isn't ready by 26 Nov, publish a Tier C video and move FingerPrint to 3 Dec. Never publish unfinished work to hit a date.

## 5. Roadmap

### Phase 0: Prepare the Christmas launch (9 Oct → 12 Nov, ~5 weeks)
Goal: everything is ready the day video 1 goes live.

| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| **1** (9–15 Oct) | Fix the money path: FingerPrint order email + Stripe live mode; check stock and **last Christmas order dates** (BE/NL/EU). Decide the Christmas bundle. Write the video 1 script. |
| **2** (16–22 Oct) | **Shoot Sloper King:** A-roll in the workshop, gym clips with 3–5 climbers (their reactions are your social proof), product photos (flat-lay, in-hand, on the cable machine). Replace the placeholders on the product page. |
| **3** (23–29 Oct) | Edit video 1. **Shoot FingerPrint:** generator screen capture, calipers, printing timelapse, wall test. Gym consignment visits start (gifts are bought in gyms in Nov/Dec). |
| **4** (30 Oct–5 Nov) | Edit video 2 and 3 Shorts per video. Thumbnails (2–3 variants each). Show video 1 to three strangers; fix where they got bored. |
| **5** (6–12 Nov) | Instagram **countdown**; early-access mail to the newsletter 24–48 h before public launch; scheduled posts; final shop test with a real order. |

**Instagram, since you've already been teasing:** turn the teaser into a countdown with one clear promise and date ("Sloper King, 12 Nov"), a "tell me when it's live" sign-up (it feeds your email list), and Stories showing batch assembly. Don't start another teaser cycle.

### Phase 1: Christmas launch (12 Nov → Jan)
Goal: first sales and proof. **Targets (goals, not promises): 500–1,500 subscribers, 300+ emails, 30–100 orders, one video above 10k views.**
- Videos 1–3 as above; 3 Shorts/Reels per video (day 0, +3, +7).
- **Gift positioning** for every post in this window: "Gift for the climber who has everything". Show the **last order date** clearly, and keep the shipping promise honest.
- Early-access reward for the list (a small first-days discount; the `FOUNDER25` idea in the Sloper King plan is available, but check it against the live prices first).
- Retention review after each video (30 min), noted in one line for the next script.
- Editor (~€300/video) from video 3 or 4, paid out of the first sales.

### Phase 2: Find the formula (Feb → Jul 2027)
Goal: learn which video type works and repeat it. **Targets: 5–10k subscribers, 1,500+ emails, a repeatable 1–3 orders per 1,000 views.**
- **One long video every 2 weeks** (alternating Tier B and C, one Tier A per quarter) plus 2–3 Shorts per week from the same footage.
- Turn winners into series ("Built to Climb", "Road to LA" debriefs).
- Second wave of products from the ideas list (custom name/logo, depth, wooden fingertips), announced to the list first.
- Gym consignment in the 6 target gyms, with a QR card that goes to a video, not a shop page.

### Phase 3: Scale on the story (Aug 2027 → LA 2028)
Goal: 25–50k subscribers is a strong outcome, 100k a stretch.
- Qualification and World Cup content as the weekly pulse; builds as the evergreen back catalogue.
- Sponsor and Leuven Partners pitch backed by the channel's real numbers.
- Big collaborations (Hannes Van Duysen, more IFSC athletes, a brand partner).
- 12 months out switch to a countdown format; the last 90 days become a documentary-style series.
- **Christmas 2027** is the second sales peak: plan products and bundle in September.

> **Decision rule at video 10:** compare the best and worst 3 videos by (a) retention at 30 s, (b) emails per 1,000 views, (c) orders per 1,000 views. Make more of the top type and stop making the bottom type.

Your account is small, so the first videos need people to bring them in:

- **Leuven network:** iVOX, KU Leuven, Leuven Partners; engineers share engineer content.

## 8. Weekly rhythm that fits a training week
- **Monday:** plan; write the next script outline (30 min).
- **Training days:** carry the phone/DJI Osmo; film 2–3 clips of anything useful (B-roll bank).
- **One build/shoot block per fortnight** (half a day), batch all A-roll and 3 Shorts at once.
- **Editor handles the cut** from video 3 on; you do the thumbnail and the final review.
- **Publish day:** fixed (e.g. Thursday 18:00 CET). Reply to every comment in the first 2 hours.
- **Competition weeks:** no long video. A Short and an Instagram story only, then a debrief video after you're home.
