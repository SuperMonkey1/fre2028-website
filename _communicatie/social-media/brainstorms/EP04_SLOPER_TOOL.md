#### Detailed Scene Breakdown:

* **Scene 1: The Sloper Paradox (00:00 – 01:15)**
  * *Visual:* 4K slow-mo peeling off a glassy, slick slope hold. Cut to elite climbers hanging +50 kg on a 20mm edge, yet failing on a 35° sloper.
  * *Hook:* Grip strength is not a single number. Why can someone with world-class crimp strength slide off a slope hold like it's covered in butter?

* **Scene 2: The Training Trap (01:15 – 03:00)**
  * *Visual:* Hangboarding, Kilter board sessions, finger hypertrophy tests.
  * *Core Insight:* Almost all modern climbing training targets isolated finger flexors (*FDP* and *FDS*). But slopers have zero positive lip—finger friction alone cannot hold bodyweight without continuous wrist stabilization torque.

* **Scene 3: The Gym Blind Test (03:00 – 05:00)**
  * *Visual:* Fré visits a local gym (De Stordeur / Bleau). Clips the raw prototype to a pull-up bar. Invites strong local crushers to hang for 15 seconds.
  * *Result:* Instant wrist extension collapse within 4 seconds. Surprise and laughs: *"My forearms are completely on fire!"*

* **Scene 4: The Biomechanics & Friction Physics (05:00 – 07:15)**
  * *Visual:* Clear animated biomechanical diagrams and friction vectors ($F_{\text{friction}} = \mu \cdot F_{\text{normal}}$).
  * *Explanation:* On a crimp, force is supported by mechanical interlock. On a sloper, you must maximize normal force across your entire palm and distal phalanges. This requires active wrist flexion driven by *Flexor Carpi Radialis* and *Flexor Carpi Ulnaris*. When those collapse into passive extension, the normal force drops to zero and you slip.

* **Scene 5: Why Existing Tools Don't Work (07:15 – 08:45)**
  * *Critique of current market:*
    * *Wooden training balls:* They spin or encourage an open-hand crimp over the top.
    * *Hangboard slopers:* Become polished ice once chalk builds up, and flat angles force awkward shoulder positions rather than natural compression.

* **Scene 6: The Build & 800 kg Destruction Rig Test (08:45 – 10:30)**
  * *Visual:* 3D printer macro timelapse (gyroid infill), failed prototype graveyard (cracked layer lines, wrong radii).
  * *The Engineering Breakthrough:* Mechanical decoupling! 4mm semi-static cord carries 100% of the tensile load (tested to 800 kg), while the 3D-printed ergonomic concave shell experiences pure compression.
  * *The Friction Surface:* Adding CROP GoldX 220-grit replaceable texture so chalk bites like sandstone without wearing down the tool.

* **Scene 7: Radical Transparency, Open Files & Pre-Order (10:30 – 12:00)**
  * *Direct-to-camera, honest & transparent:*
    1. **Pre-order Batch 1:** Limited Batch 1 manufactured, assembled, and tested locally in Leuven, Belgium (`fre2028.la/bambum` or `sloperking.com`).
    2. **Free STL for Makers:** Open-source download for anyone with a 3D printer to print their own at home.
    3. **10 Beta Testers:** Recruiting 10 climbers for a 6-week progressive overload protocol.
    4. **The Teaser Bridge (Video 2):** Hold up the custom finger-length staggered hangboard prototype: *"Wrist flexion solves slopers, but on edges, uneven finger anatomy causes pulley injuries. Next week, I'm showing how we generate custom hangboards from 3D finger scans."*
