### 1. Content Blueprint (From Master List)
* **ID:** HW-02
* **Working Title:** *Your Fingers Aren't the Same Length. So I Built an Uneven Hangboard.*
* **Alternative Titles:** 
  * *I 3D-Printed an "Uneven" Hangboard (Because Flat Rungs Make No Sense)*
  * *Your Hand Isn't Flat. Why Is Your Hangboard?*
* **Core Asset / Hardware:** The *Uneven Crimp* staggered edge, digital calipers for anatomical profiling, custom parametric 3D print generator, load cell force distribution comparison.
* **Core Question:** Look at your hand: index, middle, ring, and pinky fingers are completely different lengths, and every climber's hand anatomy is unique. Yet for 30 years, every standard hangboard has been a flat horizontal block. That forces shorter fingers (especially pinkies) into hyperextension or leaves them underloaded, while overloading the middle finger and stressing A2 pulleys. Can a custom, stepped 3D-printed edge equalize tendon recruitment?
