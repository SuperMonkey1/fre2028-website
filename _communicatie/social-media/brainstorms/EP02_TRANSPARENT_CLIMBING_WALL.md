### 4. The Triple Hook Architecture
This video is not just *"I built a weird wall."* It contains **three distinct, escalating hooks**:

1. **The Transparent Wall (Visual Spectacle):** The immediately arresting visual payoff—seeing a climber's face and beta from *inside* the wall.
2. **The Counter-Intuitive Mold-Making Method (Maker / Engineering Mystery):** An innovative, non-obvious tooling technique that challenges traditional CNC foam/resin mold making.
3. **The Manufacturing Philosophy (Thermoforming Economics & Ecology):** Minimal material use, 100% recyclable plastics, lightning-fast cycle times, and radical cost reduction.

### 6. Failure Modes to Film (Mandatory Footage Checklist)
* [ ] **Mold Failure:** Mold cracking, deforming under heat, or sticking to the hot sheet.
* [ ] **Wrinkles / Webbing:** Excess plastic folding over itself on deep-draw features.
* [ ] **Bad Heating:** Sheet too cold (poor definition) vs. sheet too hot (bubbling / loss of optical clarity).
* [ ] **Poor Vacuum / Seal Leak:** Loss of edge detail around bolt holes or hold contours.
* [ ] **Deformed Panels:** Thermal shrinkage warping the 50x50 cm square border.
* [ ] **Trimming Mistakes:** Off-axis cuts or cracked edges during mechanical routing.
* [ ] **Material Scrap:** Showing the offcuts and demonstrating the recycling loop.
