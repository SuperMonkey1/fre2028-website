### 1. Content Blueprint (From Master List)
* **ID:** HW-07 / HW-11
* **Working Title:** *Can a 3D Print Hold 200 kg? (Testing Climbing Gear to Destruction)*
* **Alternative Title:** *I Tested 3D-Printed Climbing Gear to Catastrophic Failure*
* **Core Asset / Hardware:** Static hydraulic load cell test rig, digital load readout, 1000 FPS high-speed camera, custom 3D-printed climbing rungs (PLA vs. PETG vs. Carbon-Nylon), shear and layer-adhesion fracture analysis.
* **Core Question:** Climbers constantly warn that 3D-printed climbing gear is brittle, dangerous, and prone to sudden catastrophic failure. Exactly how strong is a 3D print in shear and tension, and can a properly designed print hold 200 kg?
* **Safety Mandate:** All destructive testing to failure takes place strictly inside the hydraulic load cell rig behind acrylic shielding; zero human bodyweight risk.
