### 1. Content Blueprint (From Master List)
* **ID:** HW-04
* **Working Title:** *I Built a Prosthetic Foot That Grips Better Than a Climbing Shoe*
* **Alternative Title:** *I 3D-Printed My Own Foot to Climb On*
* **Core Asset / Hardware:** Custom bionic climbing foot, socket CAD in Fusion 360, carbon layup, high-friction climbing rubber integration, gym micro-edge testing rig.
* **Core Question:** Commercial prosthetics are designed for walking on flat pavement, not holding bodyweight on 2-millimeter crystals. Can an engineer build a custom bionic foot that outperforms commercial climbing shoes?

### 2. Packaging & Hook (Initial Concept)

* **Opening 15 Seconds (Cold Physical Hook):**
  * *Visual:* Fré in the workshop fitting the carbon-fiber prosthetic foot onto his leg. Instant cut to him stepping onto an impossibly small 2mm crystal on a steep overhang—rock-solid stability.

So as a mechanical engineer competing for Paralympic Gold, I spent 6 months in CAD building my own bionic climbing foot from scratch. Today, we test if it actually grips better than a climbing shoe."*
