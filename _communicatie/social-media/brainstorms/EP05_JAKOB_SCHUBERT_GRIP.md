### 1. Content Blueprint (From Master List)
* **ID:** COL-01
* **Working Title:** *I 3D-Printed a Custom Training Grip for World Champion Jakob Schubert*
* **Alternative Title:** *Can Engineering Make the World's Best Climber Stronger?*
* **Core Asset / Hardware:** Alginate/silicone handprint mold, handheld 3D optical scanner, Fusion 360 surface modeling, multi-material 3D print / CNC milled wood grip, custom hangboard mounting system.
* **Core Question:** Jakob Schubert is a 6-time World Champion and 2-time Olympic Bronze medalist. But commercial training grips are built for average hands, not his specific anatomical finger proportions and pulley pressure points. Can an engineer custom-scan his hand, model a bespoke edge in CAD, and create a measurably superior training grip?
* **Collaboration Ground Rule:** The video is not "Fré hangs out with Jakob." The premise is: *"Can I engineer something specifically for one of the world's best climbers?"*
