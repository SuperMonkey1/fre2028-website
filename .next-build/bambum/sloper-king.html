How a Brutal World Championship Failure Led to an Engineering Breakthrough.

My forearms pumped out completely, my fingers peeled off millimeter by millimeter, and I fell: 11th place. In the second route, I placed 2nd and made finals. • 2x World Cup Gold Medalist, KU Leuven Mechanical Engineering PhD

As an engineer and an elite athlete, I refused to just accept that limitation. Why was my performance on crimps world-class, while I struggled on slopers?

The answer was in the training tools: I had spent years training on standard hangboards and Kilterboards. But those tools isolate the fingers in a closed crimp position. When I sat down to model the biomechanics, the anatomical blind spot became obvious.

SCIENCE & ANATOMY

Why Slopers Demand 4 Muscles & Full Forearm Contraction

Traditional hangboards only load your finger flexors on crimps with a passive wrist. Sloper King™ trains your finger flexors while actively contracting both wrist flexors across all 4 primary muscles simultaneously.

ILLUSTRATION / 3D ANATOMY GRAPHIC PLACEHOLDER
What to showcase here: Forearm anatomical cross-section diagram comparing isolated finger flexors (FDP/FDS) with active wrist flexors (FCR/FCU) contracting during an open sloper grip.
Traditional Hangboard (Crimp)

Only 2 Muscles (Passive Wrist)

Fingers hook onto edges with an extended/passive wrist, leaving the wrist flexors completely disengaged.

1
Flexor digitorum profundus (FDP)
Flexes fingertips on crimp edges.
2
Flexor digitorum superficialis (FDS)
Flexes middle joints on crimp edges.
The Joint-Angle Blindspot: Muscle strength is joint-angle specific. Strength gained holding edges with an extended wrist has near-zero carryover to the active wrist contraction needed on slopers.
High peak shear stress on A2/A4 finger pulleys (injury prone).
Sloper King™ Activation

All 4 Muscles (Active Contraction)

Engages finger flexors while actively contracting both wrist flexors inward to generate true sloper friction.

1. FDP (Finger Flexor): Active finger grip tension
2. FDS (Finger Flexor): Deep isometric hold tension
3. Flexor carpi radialis (FCR): Actively contracts thumb-side wrist flexor
4. Flexor carpi ulnaris (FCU): Actively contracts pinky-side wrist flexor
Direct Sloper Transfer: Training finger flexors alongside active wrist flexion contraction transfers 1:1 to real-world slopers.
Zero lever pressure on pulleys: 100% tendon-safe deep pump training.

The Accidental Discovery: The "Pump Master"

"When I rigged the Sloper King to a cable machine at the gym, I discovered something unexpected: because 4 muscles fire under continuous isometric tension while finger joints remain open and unpinched, it delivers the most intense and tendon-safe forearm pump training I've ever experienced. You can train forearm endurance to absolute muscular failure with zero pulley injury risk."

ENGINEERING & CONSTRUCTION

Mechanically Decoupled: 800 kg Load Capacity in <100 Grams.

Rather than building a heavy, fragile 3D block, we fully decoupled the load-bearing function from the ergonomic contact surface.

PHOTO PLACEHOLDER • CAD PROTOTYPES & 100KG BREAK-TEST RIG
What to showcase here: Photo of the CAD prototype evolution next to the final production model with the 220-grit contact surface, plus a screenshot of the 100 kg pull-force load test on the semi-static cord.

800 kg Semi-Static Cord

The 4mm semi-static load cord carries 100% of the tensile load (800 kg per strand, doubled = 1,600 kg theoretical). Tested beyond 100kg+ bodyweight.

220-Grit GoldX Friction

Because plastic is too slick, the ergonomic concave shell is surfaced with CROP GoldX 220-grit grip sandpaper. A reliable, predictable friction coefficient without tearing skin.

5 Cantilever Angles

Easily shift the cord across 5 numbered positions to adjust the difficulty from a subtle open-hand surface to an aggressive, hanging sloper.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know

Why is the cord decoupled from the 3D-printed body?

3D-printed plastics can delaminate under extreme shear torque. By looping an 800 kg semi-static cord directly through the cantilever channels, the cord carries 100% of the load. The 3D print serves purely as an ergonomic, anatomical contact shell.

Can I replace the grip sandpaper when worn out?

Yes! We use CROP GoldX 220-grit sandpaper with self-adhesive backing. You simply peel off the worn strip and apply a fresh pre-cut strip in seconds.

Why train on a gym cable machine rather than regular pull-ups?

On an overhead pull-up bar, you are always constrained to 100% of your bodyweight (unless using counterweight pulleys). On a gym cable machine, you can dial in the load down to the exact kilogram (e.g. 15 kg or 35 kg). This enables targeted hypertrophy and sustained isometric fatigue sets (35–45s continuous tension).
